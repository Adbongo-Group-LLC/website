# Argument for sustainability

![](http://adbongo.files.wordpress.com/2011/03/golden-egg.png?w=300)  If your business is doing well this year,  great!  We would like you to consider that if you have not implemented a plan for sustainability you are temporary.  This year you may be fine, but according to many sources this is a false economic recovery.   Why would you test it, it is only March!?  Implement a triple p bottom line:  people, planet and profit.  If our fiat currency crashes you will still be standing on two legs.    10’s of thousands have already collapsed from not implementing this business model.
